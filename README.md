## This mod makes basic mobs harder and adds dynamic world difficulty!

### Mobs could get special Celestial Types, which overhauls their mechanics and adds new ones.

---
### Dynamic Difficulty Mode

- Normal Mode: starts as world created
- Hard Mode: starts as player enters Underworld
- Extreme Mode: starts as player enters The Nether
- Legendary Mode: starts as player kills The Wither
- Endgame Mode: starts as player kills The Ender Dragon

---
### AI Changes
* Arrow Attacks: mobs that attack with arrows attack faster the closer target it
* Animal Afraid of Tools: mobs that afraid of attackers are also afraid of tools
* Golem Targeting: mobs that target player also target iron golems
* Xray Creeper Explosions: creeper could explode even with blocked line of sight to target

---
### Other Changes
* Legendary Mode must be entered to activate End Portal Frame
* Creepers could spawn as powered as difficulty increases
* Creepers explode 2 times for blocks

---
### Celestial Types
* Skeletons (All)
  * Withered: looks darker with Wither Skeleton patterns, x2 health, attacks with fire
* Zombies (All)
  * Phase: looks Phase Spider colored, dodges attacks
  * Plague: looks infected with red dots all over the body, faster when has target, infects on attack
* Phase Spider
  * Warp: looks lime colored, on teleportation nearby hostile gets teleported to its position
* Ghoul
  * Vampire: has green glowing eyes, heals when attacks by 1.0 health or dealt damage health whichever is greater
* Shadow
  * Gloom: has red glowing eyes, speed and damage based on brightness, based on brightness could apply effects such as Wither, Nausea and Blindness
  * Spectral: skips a few path nodes in darkness
* Creepers (All)
  * Mimic: looks as block it stands on, Infernal Creeper mimics perfectly, all others with flaws
* Squid
  * Glow: looks cyan colored and glows, applies stronger effects and drowns target at contact
