## 1. Zombie Pigman
*   **Vampire:** Very high defense. Weak to wooden tools. Drops: *Gold Ingot*.
*   **Fishing:** Uses a fishing rod to catch and reel the player in. Drops: *Raw Fish*.
*   **Giant:** Bigger, possesses more health and higher damage.
*   **Hungry:** Missing stomach; eats food from the player's inventory on hit. Drops: *Apple*.
*   **Plague:** Spotted appearance; inflicts random negative status effects. Drops: *Brown/Red Mushroom*.
*   **Brutish:** Sharp teeth; higher health and damage that ignores part of the player's armor. Drops: *Flint*.

## 2. Enderman
*   **Blinding:** Fully dark black with a cross-shaped eye patch; blinds players when nearby. Drops: *Ink Sac*.
*   **Cursed:** Red complexion with Darth Maul face coloring; stacks weakness and mining fatigue on hit. Drops: *Gunpowder, Sugar, Spider Eye, Fermented Spider Eye, Glistering Melon*.
*   **Icy:** Lighter aquamarine; immune to fire and applies Slowness IV on hit. Drops: *Snowball*.
*   **Lightning:** Aquamarine color; immune to fire and summons lightning on hit. Drops: *Redstone*.
*   **Mirage:** Pale peach color; summons illusions when teleporting. Drops: *Silverfish Monster Egg*.
*   **Mini:** Tiny Enderman variant.
*   **Thief:** Bandit mask and stripes; moves much faster and teleports targets on hit.

## 3. Spider
*   **Baby:** Smaller with less health and damage.
*   **Desert:** Applies nausea, blindness, slowness, and negative resistance on hit. Drops: *Leather*.
*   **Flying:** Immune to fall damage and leaps very far. Drops: *Feather*.
*   **Giant:** Bigger with more health and damage.
*   **Mother:** Pink eyes; larger size, regenerates health, and spawns baby spiders upon death.
*   **Pale:** Applies Weakness III on hit. Drops: *Fermented Spider Eye*.
*   **Poison:** Dark blue body; inflicts poison on hit.
*   **Ghost:** Transparent and outlined; can pass through blocks. Drops: *Slimeball*.
*   **Small:** Smaller than a standard spider.
*   **Tough:** Lime and green eyes; very resistant. Drops: *Flint, Iron Ingot*.
*   **Witch:** Immune to potions and arrows; applies negative resistance on hit. Drops: *Blaze Powder, Glowstone Dust, Ghast Tear, Magma Cream*.
*   **Hungry:** Steals and eats random items from the player's inventory, then potentially despawns. Drops: *Apple, Potion of Hunger, Stolen Items*.

## 4. Creeper
*   **Armor:** Yellow color; wears a Leather Chestplate and Boots, has more health. Drops: *Leather*.
*   **Death:** Dark grey with red eyes; produces a deadly explosion.
*   **Dirt:** Brown color; spews dirt upon blowing up. Drops: *Dirt, Bread*.
*   **Doom:** Dark grey; explodes upon death, causing significant damage. Drops: *Bone Meal*.
*   **Ender:** Black with purple eyes; teleports around and burns in sunlight. Drops: *Ender Pearl*.
*   **Fire:** Red color; causes a fiery explosion that sets the environment on fire. Drops: *Coal, Fire Charge*.
*   **Jumping:** Purple color; capable of high jumps. Drops: *Glowstone*.
*   **Lightning:** Light blue; explodes into lightning. Drops: *Redstone*.
*   **Splitting:** Light green with a red-eyed face on each side of its head; a ranged attack will turn it into a mini creeper.
*   **Mini:** Tiny but fast with a small explosion.
*   **Drowning:** Dark blue with red eyes; creates a sphere of water surrounded by silverfish-laden blocks. Drops: *Bucket*.
*   **Dark:** Wears a Jack o'Lantern; its explosion breaks nearby light sources. Drops: *Torches, Potion of Night Vision*.
*   **Gravel:** Gravel texture; bursts into a pile of gravel upon exploding. Drops: *Gravel, Flint*.
*   **Gravity:** Dark purple; higher health, pulls in nearby entities as it explodes. Drops: *Gold Nugget, Apple*.

## 5. Skeleton (and Wither Skeleton)
*   **Fire:** Flaming appearance; immune to fire, shoots burning arrows. Drops: *Coal, Stone Sword (Fire Aspect I), Bow (Flame I)*.
*   **Gatling:** Goggles; fires arrows at an extremely high rate. Drops: *Gunpowder*.
*   **Brutish:** Sharp teeth; fires arrows or wields swords that pierce armor. Drops: *Flint, Bow (Agony I), Stone Sword (Pain I)*.
*   **Giant:** Big; higher health and damage.
*   **Poison:** Green appearance; fires poison arrows or uses a poison sword. Drops: *Spider Eye, Bow (Venom I), Stone Sword (Poison Aspect I)*.
*   **Ninja:** Ninja headband; camouflages as a random terrain block, performs melee attacks (sometimes with a katana) if approached or exposed. Drops: *Ink Sac, Paper, Katana (Sharpness III), Kusanagi-no-Tsurugi (Pain II & Unbreaking V)*.
*   **Thief:** Bandit eye mask; uses melee attacks and disarms the player. Drops: *Gold Nugget*.
*   **Spitfire:** Big and flaming; fires a volley of fireballs. Drops: *Fire Charge, Bow (Flame I), Stone Sword (Fire Aspect I)*.

## 6. Zombie
*   **Fire:** Flaming appearance; immune to fire and burns the player on hit. Drops: *Coal*.
*   **Fishing:** Uses a fishing rod to catch and reel the player in. Drops: *Raw Fish*.
*   **Giant:** Big zombie with increased health and damage.
*   **Hungry:** Missing stomach; eats food from the player's inventory on hit. Drops: *Apple*.
*   **Plague:** Spotted appearance; inflicts random negative status effects. Drops: *Brown/Red Mushroom*.
*   **Brutish:** Sharp teeth; higher health and armor-piercing damage. Drops: *Flint*.

## 7. Ghast
*   **Baby:** Tiny ghast that uses melee attacks instead of shooting fireballs.
*   **Fighter:** Grey with red stripes; uses melee attacks instead of fireballs. Drops: *Iron Ingot*.
*   **King:** Dark grey with golden stripes; regenerates health and shoots larger fireballs. Drops: *Gold Ingot, Emerald*.
*   **Queen:** Dark grey with purple stripes; regenerates health and spawns baby ghasts. Drops: *Gold Ingot, Emerald*.
*   **Mini:** Tiny ghast that picks up hostile mobs.
*   **Unholy:** White with a black mouth; immune to weapons without the Smite enchantment. Drops: *Gold Ingot*.
*   **Faint:** Green, transparent with an outline; phases through blocks and uses melee attacks. Drops: *Slimeball*.

## 8. Silverfish
*   **Fishing:** Red striped; uses a fishing rod to catch and reel the player in. Drops: *Raw Fish*.
*   **Blindness:** Tan with a grey head; applies blindness on hit. Drops: *Ink Sac*.
*   **Flying:** Blue color; immune to fall damage and leaps at targets. Drops: *Feather*.
*   **Tough:** Big and black with red eyes; possesses very high defense. Drops: *Flint*.
*   **Poison:** Green color; immune to poison and poisons targets on hit. Drops: *Spider Eye*.

## 9. Slime
*   **Strawberry:** Strawberry red; immune to fire and lights the player on fire. Drops: *Fire Charge, Red Dye, Potion of Fire Resistance*.
*   **Blueberry:** Blueberry blue; cannot drown and hops at normal height underwater. Drops: *Light Blue Dye, Potion of Water Breathing*.
*   **Watermelon:** Watermelon green; slings itself at targets and deals knockback. Drops: *Melon (Item), Lime Dye, Potion of Resistance*.
*   **Blackberry:** Blackberry black; higher health, explodes like a creeper when close. Drops: *Gunpowder, Ink Sac*.
*   **Grape:** Grape purple; immune to fall damage and leaps at targets. Drops: *Purple Dye, Potion of Jump Boost*.
*   **Lemon:** Lemon yellow; immune to fire, summons lightning on hit, and teleports away. Drops: *Redstone, Yellow Dye, Potion of Haste*.
*   **Caramel:** Caramel orange; higher health, makes targets stick to it while dealing constant damage. Drops: *Sugar, Orange Dye, Sticky Sword (Sharpness III)*.

## 10. Blaze
*   **Inferno:** Orange color; higher health, shoots a barrage of fireballs. Drops: *Blaze Powder, Fire Charge*.
*   **Hellfire:** Gray with red eyes; higher health, shoots a single Ghast fireball. Drops: *Gunpowder*.
*   **Smolder:** Black with 6 eyes; blinds the target. Drops: *Coal*.
*   **Ember:** Black with yellow eyes; rushes the target with melee attacks. Drops: *Coal*.
*   **Cinder:** Tiny Blaze; rushes the target with melee and ignites them on hit.
*   **Conflagration:** Black rods, normal head with red eyes; very high defense, weak to snowballs. Drops: *Fire Charge, Potion of Fire Resistance*.
*   **Jolt:** Blue with orange eyes; strikes lightning and teleports when hit, dodges projectiles. Drops: *Redstone*.
*   **Wildfire:** Big Blaze with 4 red eyes; high health, alternates between fireballs and spawning cinders (rushes with melee at low health). Spawns cinders upon death. Drops: *Coal, Blaze Spawn Egg*.

## 11. Witch
*   **Witch of the Wind:** Darker purple robe; drinks speed and rushes with melee, can use invisibility and teleportation. Drops: *Feather, Potion of Hiding*.
*   **Witch of the Wilds:** Green robe; starts riding a spider, heals the spider, throws poison potions, and spawns baby spiders. Drops: *Fermented Spider Eye, Skeleton Spawn Egg*.
*   **Witch of Shadows:** Black robe and white eyes; throws blindness potions. Drops: *Ink Sac, Splash Potion of Blindness*.
*   **Witch of the Undead:** Black torn robe with a zombie villager face; counts as undead, spawns skeleton minions, and heals via instant damage potions. Drops: *Skeleton Spawn Egg, Skeleton Skull*.
*   **Witch of Rage:** Brown robe; drinks strength and resistance potions to melee targets, refreshing them when they expire. Drops: *Blaze Powder, Potion of Rage*.
*   **Witch of Domination:** Fancy robe with a yellow outline; uses melee attacks and inflicts wither on hit. Drops: * Bottle o' Enchanting, Helmet of Mind Protection*.
